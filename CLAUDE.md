# Claude guidance

Read `AGENTS.md` before making changes. This is the public corporate website for United Dream Limited, not an individual product site.

Keep the site compatible with static GitHub Pages hosting. Do not introduce server-only APIs, secrets, or runtime environment dependencies. Prefer accessible, fast, progressively enhanced pages and keep corporate claims factual and reviewable.

Template assets may only be committed after their Envato license has been confirmed for this project. Preserve required license records and attribution notices.
